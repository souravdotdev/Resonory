# Waitlist

Who is waiting for Resonory to open, collected by the waitlist page before launch.

## Language

**Waitlist Entry**:
An email address that asked for early access to Resonory. It is not a User: it has no sign-in identity and owns nothing.
_Avoid_: Subscriber, lead, signup

**Welcome email**:
The one message sent to a new Waitlist Entry when they join. It confirms they are on the list and says they will get one more email when early access opens. Signing up again with the same address sends nothing.
_Avoid_: Confirmation email, verification email, opt-in email
