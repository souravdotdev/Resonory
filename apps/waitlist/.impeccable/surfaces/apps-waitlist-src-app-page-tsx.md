# Waitlist landing page

**Scope and mode:** the public waitlist landing page at resonory.com (apps/waitlist). Persuade.

**Audience and job:** a first-time visitor from X, Reddit/HN or a friend's link. They need to grasp in about five seconds that Resonory brings back what they saved, then leave their email. The page carries one action (join the waitlist), offered in the hero and again at the close.

**Proof and content:** no testimonials, numbers or logos. The page proves the idea by demonstrating it: its own search answered by the visitor's own forgotten saves. Demo content is synthetic and believable. The claims follow PRODUCT.md's allowed and banned lists.

**Constraints:**

- The colours are fixed, the form is email only, and the logo motif is the orange dot rising from the stack.
- The page order is the one approved in #100, and the founder note needs the founder's sign-off.
- The drift layer is slow, never sits behind text, stays still under reduced motion and doesn't react to the cursor.

**Memorable moment:** a query types itself into a huge palette, and the top result is something "you saved in March", lit orange, the logo's dot.

**Unresolved:** the founder's edits to the founder note.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Resonory is the search box that answers with your own forgotten saves. This page refuses the centered-headline-over-screenshot-plus-bento arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is #141414. Floating palettes are #1F1F1F/#272727 with hairline light borders. Results are rows with item-type glyphs, and keycaps (⌘K, ↵) and meta are set in Geist Mono. Orange marks only a save that comes back.

STORY: The visitor watches a search surface something they saved and forgot. They believe Resonory finds things without being asked, and they join.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The headline sits top left. Below it, the email field is a palette input with an ↵ keycap. On the right, at two-thirds width, a giant palette types "agile retrospective". Its top section, "From your saves", lifts one row orange as the logo's dot. Saved cards drift at the edges.

FORM: Command Palette, my first-ranked grounded candidate (IMPECCABLE'S PICK). Seed 6ed54b15.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
