# Waitlist single screen

**Scope and mode:** the public waitlist page at resonory.com (apps/waitlist), one screen with no scrolling. Persuade.

**Audience and job:** a first-time visitor from X, Reddit/HN or a friend's link. They need to grasp in about five seconds that Resonory brings back what they saved, then leave their email. The screen carries one action (join the waitlist): the form, and the nav button that focuses it.

**Proof and content:** no testimonials, numbers or logos. The screen proves the idea by demonstrating it: a search answered by a forgotten save that shares its meaning, not its words. Demo content is synthetic and believable, with no real brands. The claims follow PRODUCT.md's allowed and banned lists.

**Constraints:**

- The founder's Claude Design artboards (1440×900 and 375×812) are the authority: colours, sizes, the orange "saved" and "brings", the traffic lights.
- Two fluid compositions, desktop from 1024px and mobile below; each fills one dynamic viewport, and only a screen too short for the form scrolls.
- The colours are fixed, the form is email only, and the logo motif is the orange dot rising from the stack. Geist and Geist Mono come from the bundled package.
- The wall drifts slowly, never sits behind the copy, form, demo, nav or footer, stays still under reduced motion and doesn't react to the cursor.

**Memorable moment:** a search types itself, one card on the wall of forgotten saves lights orange, a thread runs to it from the Resonory icon, and a notice says "You saved something like this".

**Unresolved:** none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Resonory remembers what you saved and forgot. One screen, no sections: watch it happen.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #141414, a dimmed wall of saved cards (#1F1F1F, hairline borders). Panels #1F1F1F/#272727; meta and keycaps in Geist Mono. Orange marks only the two verbs and a save that comes back.

STORY: The visitor sees their own forgotten saves, watches a search find one by meaning, believes Resonory brings things back unasked, and joins.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A pill nav on top. Left: the headline, white to grey, with orange "saved" and "brings", the sub, and an email field styled as a search with an ↵ keycap. Right: a browser window searching "agile retrospective ideas" and the notification. A footer pill below.

FORM: The founder's Claude Design single screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
